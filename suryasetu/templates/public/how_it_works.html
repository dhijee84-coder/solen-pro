THE JOURNEY

How Solan works

You buy panels on our farm. We run the plant. You pay for the energy those panels generate. Nothing is installed on your house.

Register

Create a client account with your mobile number and OTP.

Complete KYC

PAN and Aadhaar so panel ownership is in your name.

Share usage

Electricity bill details help size how many farm panels you need.

Choose a farm

You are linked to an active Solan site on open land.

Buy panels

Available modules are allocated from farm inventory (first-in, first-out).

We install on our land

Solan crews mount, cable and commission your panels at the farm — never on your roof.

Pay for the panels

Milestone payments for the purchase; farm work stays our job.

Grid & handover

Inspection and grid connection at the farm. You receive generation rights.

Energy on usage

Track daily generation. You are billed for the energy your panels produce.

We maintain

Cleaning, repairs, warranty claims and support are handled by Solan.
