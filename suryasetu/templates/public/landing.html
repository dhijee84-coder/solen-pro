Get started

How allocation works

1. Register & verify

Sign up with your mobile number, confirm PAN and Aadhaar, and upload your electricity bill.

2. Get allocated capacity

We size a plant for your usage and allocate panels from an active SuryaSetu project on a first-in, first-out basis.

3. Track installation

Follow six installation stages from sign-off to full commissioning, with payments tied to each milestone.

4. Monitor generation

See daily, monthly and lifetime generation from your allocated capacity, along with CO₂ avoided.
