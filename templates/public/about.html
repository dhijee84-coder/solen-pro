ABOUT US

Solar farms on open land. Panels you own.

WHO WE ARE

Solan

Solan develops solar farms on empty land. Clients buy panels at those farms. We install, maintain and operate the plant. You never host equipment on your house — you buy capacity, then pay for the energy those panels generate.

Mission

Let people own solar capacity without rooftop construction. Fair panel sales, farm-side operations, usage-based energy billing.

Vision

A trusted farm operator: our land, your panels, our maintenance, your generation.

What we do not do

We do not mount panels on homes, factories or rooftops. All hardware stays on Solan farms.
